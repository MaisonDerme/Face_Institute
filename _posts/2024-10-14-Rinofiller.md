# Rinofiller
Rinofiller also known as a "liquid nose job," is a non-surgical cosmetic treatment that uses hyaluronic acid injections to temporarily reshape the nose, fixing minor imperfections like bumps, asymmetries, or a droopy tip, offering an alternative to surgery with minimal downtime. Performed in-office with topical anesthetic, it smooths the nasal profile, lifts the tip, and creates a more balanced look, with results lasting typically 6-12 months before needing touch-ups.
