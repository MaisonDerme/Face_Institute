# Botulinum Injection in Aesthetics Medicine
Botulinum Injection, also known as Botox treatment, uses Botulinum Toxin Type A, a neurotoxin from bacteria, injected in tiny doses to temporarily relax muscles or block nerve signals, primarily for reducing facial wrinkles (crow's feet, frown lines) but also for medical issues like chronic migraines, excessive sweating, and muscle spasms (cervical dystonia, cerebral palsy). It works by preventing nerve signals to muscles, causing temporary relaxation/paralysis, with effects lasting 3-6 months, and it's a popular, safe procedure when done by a professional.
